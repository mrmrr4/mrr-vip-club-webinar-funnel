Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BOVI MARKETING LLC, operating the MR MRR brand ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our webinar pages, register for a webinar, or communicate with us.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Registration information, such as your name, email address, mobile phone number, selected webinar session, and related registration details.
  • SMS consent records, including the mobile number used to opt in, the date and time of consent, the consent language presented, and related technical records used to document consent.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, browser/device information, page activity, referral source, and similar analytics or security data.
  • Communications you send to us, including support requests and replies.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • register you for and operate the VIP Club Masterclass;
  • send webinar access information and reminders;
  • send one SMS webinar reminder before your selected webinar session when you separately opt in to SMS;
  • provide support and respond to requests;
  • secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve our websites and webinar experience;
  • measure performance and understand how visitors use our pages; and
  • comply with legal, regulatory, carrier, and platform requirements.

3. SMS privacy and consent

SMS is used for webinar reminders only. Opting in to SMS does not enroll you in promotional or ongoing marketing text messages.

If you provide a mobile number and separately consent to SMS, you may receive up to one SMS reminder per webinar registration, generally shortly before your selected session. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may provide information to service providers that process data only as needed to deliver the webinar, communications, hosting, security, analytics, or other services on our behalf. This can include providers such as webinar, SMS, hosting, and email infrastructure vendors. These providers may process information only for the services they perform for us and subject to their applicable terms and privacy obligations.

4. Cookies and analytics

Our websites may use cookies, pixels, or similar technologies for essential site functionality, analytics, security, attribution, and measuring campaign performance. You can control some cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including operating the webinar, maintaining consent records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, meeting carrier or platform requirements, and complying with applicable law.

6. Your choices

You may unsubscribe from email communications using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in those emails. For SMS, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. You may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-retention requirements.

7. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

Our webinar and services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.
