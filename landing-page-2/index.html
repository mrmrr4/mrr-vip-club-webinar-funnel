Do I need experience in ecom?

Yes and no. If you have never run traditional ecom stores or run ads online before, this is not for you. New to MRR? That's fine. We don't help with your front end. It is your job to build your store and run traffic.

Do I need to be a US citizen?

No. The blueprint covers the setup process used by non-US operators as well.

I already have a Shopify store. Can I convert it into a VIP Club store?

Absolutely. The blueprint covers both building from scratch and converting an existing ecommerce store.

I tried dropshipping before and got burned. How is this different?

If you've run traditional dropshipping before, you already understand the hardest part: finding products and acquiring customers. VIP Club changes what happens after the sale. Instead of starting from zero every month, each new customer can become a monthly paying subscriber. That means you can focus on acquiring customers around breakeven instead of forcing huge front-end ROAS. The real game becomes building and automating a backend that can retain subscribers, process rebills, recover declines and let you scale aggressively.
